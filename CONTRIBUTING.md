## Outside contributions

**We do not merge pull requests from outside contributors yet.** Before we can, every contributor must sign a contributor agreement. The agreement grants the copyright holder a licence to your contribution, so the project can keep offering it under its open-source licence and under a commercial licence. The agreement is being prepared. Until it is published here, please open an issue to report a bug or discuss an idea instead of sending a pull request.
